Contact

We welcome thoughtful correspondence, corrections, and pitches. Write to us — a human reads every message.

Fresh Architectural Thinking is an independent editorial publication. We are a small team, and we read everything that arrives in our inbox — whether it is a detailed correction, a story tip, a question about a project, or a note about how an article helped you.

For pitches, please include a short summary and a link to previous work. We are especially interested in stories about materials, passive design, small-space planning, and the practical realities of building or renovating.
