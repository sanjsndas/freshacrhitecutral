Cookies Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on our website, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 1 March 2025

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to improve user experience, and to provide information to the site owners.

We also use similar technologies such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage. In this policy, we refer to all of these as "cookies".

2. How we use cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies to ensure our website functions properly and to understand how readers use our content. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

The cookies we use fall into the following categories:

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions made by you, such as setting your privacy preferences or filling in forms. You can set your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some parts of the site may not work as a result.

Analytics cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us to know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the site. All information these cookies collect is aggregated and therefore anonymous. We use a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not track individual users across websites.

Functionality cookies

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalisation, such as remembering your preferences (e.g., language or region). They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you do not allow these cookies, some or all of these services may not function properly.

3. Specific cookies we use

Below is a list of the specific cookies we may set, along with their purpose and duration.

Cookie name Type / Purpose Duration
fat_consent Strictly necessary — stores your cookie consent preferences. 12 months
fat_session Strictly necessary — maintains your session while navigating the site. Session
_pk_id Analytics — privacy-focused visitor identifier (anonymised). 13 months
_pk_ses Analytics — short-lived session data for analytics. 30 minutes
fat_prefs Functionality — remembers display preferences (e.g., font size). 6 months

We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting, or cross-site tracking. Our analytics are configured to anonymise IP addresses and respect your privacy.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies, and they are subject to the respective third party's privacy and cookie policies. We currently use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Google Fonts — for displaying typography. Google may collect your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Unsplash — for hosting images. Unsplash may collect anonymous usage data. See Unsplash's Privacy Policy.

We do not use social media tracking pixels or advertising networks.

5. Your cookie choices

You have several options to manage or disable cookies:

  • Cookie consent banner — when you first visit our site, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies via our banner. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer (or clearing your browser cookies and revisiting).
  • Browser settings — most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you disable cookies, some parts of the site may not function properly.
  • Opt-out tools — for analytics cookies, you can often opt out via the provider's opt-out mechanism. Since we use a privacy-focused analytics tool, no personal data is tracked, but you may still block cookies via your browser.

To learn more about cookies and how to manage them, visit aboutcookies.org or allaboutcookies.org.

6. Do Not Track signals

Some browsers support a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that sends a signal to websites indicating you do not wish to be tracked. We do not currently respond to DNT signals because there is no common industry standard for how to interpret them. However, we do not track you across third-party websites, and our analytics are anonymised by design.

7. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

8. More information

For more details on how we handle your personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.
